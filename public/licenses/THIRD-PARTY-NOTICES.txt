Liturgical and scriptural texts

The 1979 Book of Common Prayer, the King James Version and the World English
Bible (British Edition) are in the public domain and carry no restrictions.
Their provenance notes are in src/lib/content/vendor/bcp, kjv and web. The
Prayer Book is additionally licensed for reproduction by the Church Pension
Fund; the public-domain E-text edition is the basis used here.

Lesser Feasts and Fasts calendar

The fixed-date commemorations in src/lib/content/vendor/lff/calendar.min.json
are the calendar of Lesser Feasts and Fasts 2024, published for The Domestic
and Foreign Missionary Society of The Protestant Episcopal Church in the
United States of America. Only the factual calendar (names and dates) is
reproduced; the book's biographies, collects, psalms, lessons and prefaces are
not. The source, the omissions and the copyright notice are recorded in the
provenance note beside the file.
