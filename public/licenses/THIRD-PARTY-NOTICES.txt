Liturgical and scriptural texts

The 1979 Book of Common Prayer, the King James Version (including the Apocrypha
from the eBible.org KJVA module, courtesy of the CrossWire Bible Society) and
the World English Bible (British Edition) are in the public domain and carry no
restrictions. Their provenance notes are in src/lib/content/vendor/bcp, kjv,
kjva and web. The Prayer Book is additionally licensed for reproduction by the
Church Pension Fund; the public-domain E-text edition is the basis used here.

Lesser Feasts and Fasts calendar

The fixed-date commemorations in src/lib/content/vendor/lff/calendar.min.json
are the calendar of Lesser Feasts and Fasts 2024, published for The Domestic
and Foreign Missionary Society of The Protestant Episcopal Church in the
United States of America. Only the factual calendar (names and dates) is
reproduced; the book's biographies, collects, psalms, lessons and prefaces are
not. The source, the omissions and the copyright notice are recorded in the
provenance note beside the file.

1662 Book of Common Prayer

The 1662 content is drawn from public-domain historical sources: 1669 and 1770
printings held by the Internet Archive for the lectionary and psalm tables, and
public-domain Church of England texts checked against a 1762 Baskerville
facsimile for the offices. Readable online transcriptions served only as
cross-checks. Provenance and verification notes are in
src/lib/content/vendor/bcp1662/PROVENANCE.md.

1962 Book of Common Prayer (Canada)

Text is based on the Book of Common Prayer (1962) of the General Synod of the
Anglican Church of Canada, which the ACC has confirmed is in the public domain.
This project is independent and is not endorsed by the Anglican Church of
Canada. For the official publication, visit www.anglican.ca/texts. Provenance
is in src/lib/content/vendor/bcp1962/PROVENANCE.md.
